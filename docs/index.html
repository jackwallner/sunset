A score for tonight's sunset

Know before you go.

Sunset reads the cloud, humidity and visibility forecast for the minute the sun goes down and turns it into a 0 to 100 score, with an alert before the evenings worth going out for.

See how it works
01

Color and streaks

High and mid cloud around half cover is what lights up. Thin cirrus with nothing underneath makes the streaks.

02

An open horizon

The last light arrives from the west, so Sunset checks the low cloud 80 km toward the sun, not just overhead.

03

Clean, dry air

Low humidity and long visibility keep the tones vivid. Haze and rain wash them out, and the score says so.

Sunset+

Alerts for the great ones

Pick a score threshold and how far ahead you want to hear. Alerts are rebuilt as the forecast changes, so a day that clouds over loses its alert. Tonight and tomorrow stay free; Sunset+ adds alerts and the full week.

What this is not

A forecast, not a promise

Scores come from the Open-Meteo weather models. They are a good guide and an honest one, and they will sometimes be wrong. Only rounded coordinates leave your phone, and nothing is tracked or stored.
