A score for every sunrise and sunset

Know before you go.

Sunset reads the cloud, humidity and visibility forecast for the minute the sun comes up or goes down and turns it into a 0 to 100 score, with a free alert before the ones worth going out for.

Color and streaks

High and mid cloud around half cover is what lights up. Thin cirrus with nothing underneath makes the streaks.

02

An open horizon

The light arrives from the east at sunrise and the west at sunset, so Sunset checks the low cloud 80 km toward the sun instead of only overhead.

03

Clean, dry air

Low humidity and long visibility keep the tones vivid. Haze and rain wash them out, and the score says so.

Tonight's sky

How do you know if the sunset will be good tonight?

Look for high and mid cloud around half cover, a clear horizon toward the sun and dry, clean air. Sunset checks all three for where you are and scores each sunrise and sunset from 0 to 100.

Free alerts, and Sun+

Is there an app that alerts you to a good sunset?

Yes. Sunset sends a free alert before the sunrises and sunsets that score well, and rebuilds it as the forecast changes, so a sky that clouds over loses its alert. Sun+ adds the full week, alerts before thunderstorms, rainbow chances and fog, and your own alert score and lead time.

What this is not

How accurate is a sunset forecast?

A sunset score is a good guide, not a guarantee. Scores come from the Open-Meteo weather models, and they will sometimes be wrong. Only rounded coordinates leave your phone, and nothing is tracked or stored.
