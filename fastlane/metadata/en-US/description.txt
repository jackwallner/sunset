Sunset tells you how good tonight's sunset should be, hours before it happens, so you can be outside for the great ones instead of hearing about them tomorrow.

EVERY EVENING GETS A SCORE
Sunset reads the cloud, humidity and visibility forecast for the exact minute the sun goes down and turns it into a 0 to 100 score with a plain-English summary: thin high clouds over an open horizon, a bank of low cloud to the west, rain at sunset, or a clear sky and a clean gold fade.

WHY TONIGHT WILL OR WON'T LIGHT UP
Four factors show what is driving the score:
• Color: high and mid clouds that catch the light
• Streaks: thin cirrus with room to glow
• Horizon: open sky toward the sun, including the low cloud 80 km to the west that decides whether the light gets through at all
• Clarity: dry, clean air for vivid tones

PRIVACY
Sunset asks for your approximate location once and sends only rounded coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service to fetch the forecast. No account, no tracking, no analytics.

Scores are a forecast of cloud, humidity and visibility, not a promise of a sunset.
