Sunset scores every sunrise and sunset, hours before it happens, so you can be outside for the great ones instead of hearing about them later.

EVERY SUNRISE AND SUNSET GETS A SCORE
Sunset reads the cloud, humidity and visibility forecast for the exact minute the sun comes up or goes down and turns it into a 0 to 100 score with a plain-English summary: thin high clouds over an open horizon, a bank of low cloud toward the sun, rain at the moment, or a clear sky and a clean gold glow. Watch sunrises, sunsets, or both.

WHY THE SKY WILL OR WON'T LIGHT UP
Four factors show what is driving the score:
• Color: high and mid clouds that catch the light
• Streaks: thin cirrus with room to glow
• Horizon: open sky toward the sun, including the low cloud 80 km to the east or west that decides whether the light gets through at all
• Clarity: dry, clean air for vivid tones

FREE ALERTS
A notification before the sunsets worth going out for, and the evening before a great sunrise, so you can set an alarm. Alerts are rebuilt as the forecast changes, so a sky that clouds over loses its alert.

BEST LIGHT AND WIDGET
See each sunrise and sunset time and the window when the color peaks, for wherever you are. A Home Screen widget shows the next score.

PRIVACY
Sunset asks for your approximate location once and sends only rounded coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service to fetch the forecast. No account, no tracking, no analytics.

Scores and sky events are forecasts of cloud, humidity, visibility and rain, not a promise of what the sky will do. Stay indoors during thunderstorms.
